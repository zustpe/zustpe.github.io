Our group companies

The ZustPe Ecosystem brings together innovation, technology, and social impact through three independent yet purpose-driven organizations. Each entity focuses on a unique mission while collectively contributing to building a stronger digital and social infrastructure.

ZustPe Payment

2022

Founded in 2022, ZustPe Payments Private Limited is a fintech startup focused on delivering simplified digital payment infrastructure for businesses. With a transparent flat-rate payment gateway model, prepaid payment instruments, and white-label fintech solutions, we empower startups and enterprises to build scalable financial ecosystems for payments, cash management, gift cards, and vendor transactions.

ZustPe Technologies

2024

Established in 2024, ZustPe Technologies Private Limited is an information technology company specializing in system integration and digital infrastructure. Working with 28+ regulators across India, we build secure, scalable solutions across hospitality, healthcare, BFSI, education, telecommunications, and more—helping organizations adopt safe, innovative, and future-ready digital systems.

ZustPe Achievers Foundation

2025

Founded in 2025, ZustPe Achievers Foundation is a Section 8 non-profit committed to building meaningful social impact. Through initiatives in food support, education, and startup enablement, we work to uplift communities, empower individuals with career opportunities, and support aspiring entrepreneurs in transforming ideas into sustainable ventures.
